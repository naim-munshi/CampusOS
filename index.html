Privacy and backup

CampusOS stores tasks, notes and theme preferences in your browser's localStorage. It does not create an account, sync between devices or upload your content. Do not store passwords or sensitive personal information here.

Import replaces existing local data after confirmation. Backups are unencrypted files: handle them carefully.
